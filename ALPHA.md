## Next seven days: September 17–23

**1. One creature, one bad market mood.** Start with the GPU, paper crown, pager and ESC key. Ask for one-line captions and see which detail people actually recognise. On September 17–18, try two specific prompts instead of adding more traits.

**2. Same chart, different creatures.** On September 19–21, make requested solo cards manually, then try a Bear × Bull card with two willing people. A friend is optional. Both people approve a shared card before it is published; handles and sharing are optional. Captions stay outside the original artwork and never change NFT traits.

**3. Does the creature arrive intact?** On September 22–23, test the actual onchain media read on mobile and compare available RPC routes. Record failures as well as successes. Contract readback and marketplace display are separate checks.

These are planned experiments. There is no finished card generator, invitation app or published media-performance benchmark. We will post what happened—including no responses—rather than turn our own examples into “community activity.”
